FAQ

Where do I go to participate in the workshops, talks, raffles, and activities/mini-events?
Unless otherwise noted on the Schedule, all workshops, talks, raffles, and activities/mini-events will be held in the RMC Grand Hall!

Where can I find the HackRice team if I need help?
A HackRice team member will always be available on Discord to help and answer any questions. You can also find us in RMC wearing navy or maroon quarterzips throughout the day!

Where can I find the mentors?
You can find mentors throughout the RMC Grand Hall or on Discord!

What are the wristbands for?
Every participant will be given a wristband upon check-in to wear throughout the weekend. This is to confirm that you are officially a registered participant and are allowed to be in the venue. You will additionally need to show your wristband before you can get food at each meal.
HACKERS will have wristbands that are BLACK, BROWN, BLUE, GREEN, RED, and PINK.
SPONSORS will have wristbands that are YELLOW.
MENTORS & JUDGES will have wristbands that are ORANGE.
There are a few other volunteers who will have specially-marked pink wristbands.

Workshops

First Timers Workshop - Friday @ 8:30 pm (Farnsworth Pavilion)
Join us for the First Timers Workshop! Begin with an overview of the development process, from concept to creation. Learn how to build an effective team, plan your technical approach, and design fullstack apps! Perfect for beginners and aspiring developers eager to turn their ideas into reality!

MathWorks Workshop - Saturday @ 9:30 am (Keck Hall 100)
Agentic Engineering: When AI Stops Suggesting and Starts Doing
What if an AI teammate could turn your idea into working, tested engineering results? This session introduces agentic AI and demonstrates how coding agents can use MATLAB and Simulink to write code, build models, run simulations, diagnose errors, and verify outcomes. Through a live lung-ventilator modeling demo, students will see how natural-language intent becomes an actionable data analysis and modeling workflow. Learn how agentic tools can accelerate hackathon development, while keeping creativity, engineering judgment, and accountability firmly in your hands.

Persona Workshop - Saturday @ 2:15 pm (Farnsworth Pavilion)
Persona's Resume & Interview Prep Workshop
Your resume is your first impression—make it count. In Persona's Resume and Interview prep workshop, we’ll cover how to highlight your skills, projects, and experiences so they stand out to employers. Along the way, I’ll also share practical tips for interview prep and how to tell your story with confidence.

MLH - Intro to Google AI Studio Workshop - Saturday @ 3:30 pm (Farnsworth Pavilion)
Google AI Studio is the fastest way to start building with the Gemini family of multimodal generative AI models. Google AI Studio allows you to try out Gemini's massive token context window, grab an API key in seconds, and experiment out prebuilt prompts.

MLH - Hacking with GitHub Copilot Workshop - Saturday @ 4:15 pm (Farnsworth Pavilion)
Learning to use AI throughout your development flow is now an essential skill. GitHub Copilot is a fully-agentic AI pair programmer that can help you write, debug, & understand code. Today we’re learning by doing. We'll fork a README for your personal GitHub profile. We'll then use the GitHub and MLH MCP servers to pull live, personalized data. GitHub Copilot will use the template and data to create a customized profile just for you.

MLH - Tech Together Meetups - Saturday @ 5:00 pm (Farnsworth Pavilion)
The meetups will provide attendees with opportunities to connect, share ideas, and discuss industry challenges at the hackathon. We aim to enhance the overall hackathon experience and promote an inclusive atmosphere. These meetups will be targeted to underrepresented gender participants and their allies at hackathons.

Healthcare Track
Longevity has become one of the biggest trends in healthcare. For years, the focus was simply on lifespan, but the real goal now is healthspan—not just living longer, but staying healthy for more of that life. Longevity isn't a single problem to solve; it's a stack of layers that build on one another. At the base is molecular and cellular aging—cellular senescence, inflammation, DNA damage. On top of that sit biomarkers and diagnostics, which let us track biological age. Above those are the everyday lifestyle factors: sleep, nutrition, exercise, and stress, and at the outermost layer, environment and social connection. Every layer shapes how long someone actually stays healthy.
This track invites you to design and prototype a user-facing application that ties these layers together, turning complex science into clear, actionable guidance that helps people understand their biological age and take real steps to extend their healthspan.
Example directions

- Health and wellness tracking powered by personal health and lifestyle data.
- Personalized recommendations to support healthier habits and long-term well-being.
- Educational insights that make complex health and longevity concepts easy to understand.
- Goal-setting and coaching tools that help users build sustainable healthy behaviors.
  Example projects
- FAIR — personal air-quality guardian; covers the environment layer of the longevity stack (HackRice 15)
- DocuLabubu — assistant that helps you remember and understand doctor visits (HackRice 15)
  Prize: Air Fryer

Finance Track
Managing money is something almost everyone struggles with, yet most people lack tools that actually fit how they live and spend. The Finance track challenges you to build personal wealth management solutions—budgeting, money tracking, and planning tools that help people understand their finances and make better decisions.
Create an app or website that helps individuals take control of their money. The best projects don't just display numbers; they turn raw financial data into personalized, actionable insight, meeting people where they are and helping them build healthier financial habits over time.
Example directions

- Budgeting and expense tracking that categorizes spending automatically and surfaces where the money actually goes.
- Personalized insights and recommendations—spotting trends, flagging overspending, and suggesting realistic ways to save.
- Goal-based planning for things like an emergency fund, a big purchase, paying down debt, or saving for the future.
- Smart money tools such as bill reminders, subscription tracking, or simple forecasting of cash flow.
  Example Projects
- OwlNudge — timely nudges from your spending that save money and prevent fees (HackRice 15)
- Swipe Coach — links your cards, tracks spending, and tells you which card maximizes cashback per purchase, plus monthly insights (HackRice 15)

Judging Guidelines
Technical Rigor
We are looking for technically advanced solutions to technically challenging problems that make use of hardware and/or APIs. However, making an extensive solution with integrated simple technologies is equally impressive!
Originality & Creativity
We are looking for original ideas or new angles on existing ideas. For example, using an API in a unique way. We know APIs work—we want to see if you can do something new and awesome with them!
User Experience & Design
We are looking for aesthetically pleasing projects with a clear and intuitive user experience.
Practicality & Impact
We are looking for impactful projects that solve real-world problems. Additionally, we would like to emphasize the importance of the scope of your project as well as the scalability of your solution.
Relevance
If you submit your project to a track or challenge, we are looking for relevance to the spirit/requirements of that track/challenge.
